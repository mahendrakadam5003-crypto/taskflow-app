Good to know

Frequently asked questions

How does per-seat pricing work?

Each employee seat uses the rate for the published tier that covers your team's seat count. When a volume tier applies, its rate applies to every seat in that quote. The estimator shows the current tier and total.

Is there a discount for yearly billing?

Yearly rates are shown from the current published pricing. Any saving displayed is calculated from the configured monthly and yearly rates; check the pricing cards and estimate for your tier.

Are the displayed prices tax-inclusive?

The pricing section uses the current tax settings and states whether tax is included or added. The estimate breaks the tax amount out separately.

Will requesting a demo create an account or start a trial?

No. Demo requests are reviewed manually. They do not create an account or trial; any access requires separate approval and setup.

How do payment and onboarding work?

Onboarding and payment are handled manually. A TaskFlow administrator reviews your request and confirms the plan and invoice details before setup.

Does TaskFlow track employee location?

Location features are used only where enabled. Location is device-reported and may be inaccurate or spoofed; it is not proof of physical presence. Employers should provide employees with an appropriate notice and policy. Read the employee data notice.

Can existing companies keep using their workspace?

Yes. Existing companies continue to use their current workspace and sign in at Workspace sign in. Published pricing changes do not automatically replace an existing company's saved pricing.
