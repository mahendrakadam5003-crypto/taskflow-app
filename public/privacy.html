Demo request privacy notice

This notice describes information submitted through TaskFlow's public demo request form. It does not replace any privacy notice that your organization must provide to its employees when using TaskFlow.

Information collected

The form collects your name, work email, company name, team size, and any optional phone number or message you provide. The service also processes ordinary request metadata needed to operate and protect the form, such as your IP address for rate limiting.

How it is used

A TaskFlow administrator reviews the request and may use the submitted contact details to respond, discuss a product demonstration, and assess whether the service fits your organization. Submission does not create an account, start a trial, or initiate payment. Trial access, if offered, requires a separate manual approval and setup.

Access and storage

Demo request records are stored in TaskFlow's configured control database and are available to authorized TaskFlow super-admins for request review. The service does not send a demo request automatically to a marketing platform. Records are not automatically deleted by the demo request workflow.

Your choices

Do not include sensitive personal information in the optional message. To ask about a request or seek correction or deletion, reply through the contact channel from which TaskFlow contacted you, or contact the TaskFlow service operator directly. The operator will review the request and applicable recordkeeping requirements.

Contact and service details

TaskFlow is a software service; the organization operating a particular deployment is responsible for identifying its legal entity, contact information, and any applicable regional requirements. This notice is a description of the current demo form workflow, not legal advice or a guarantee of regulatory compliance.
